StudyMate AI

Problem: Students' notes, slides, and timetables live in separate places, with no single system that turns a weekly schedule into an actual daily study routine.

What I did: Built an AI-assisted study companion on the MERN stack — a timetable-driven routine engine, PDF material upload (outlines/slides), and an AI tutor/Q&A layer powered by the Gemini API.
